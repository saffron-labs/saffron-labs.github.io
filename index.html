What we will not make

The list we hand every client.

We use AI to make the work, and we say so on the ad. That is going to be the rule in Indian advertising soon, and we would rather be early than corrected. It also means there are things we turn down, even when a client asks:

  • Customer testimonials from people who do not exist
  • Doctors, lawyers or experts we invented
  • Before-and-after results we cannot stand behind
  • Real places dressed up as somewhere they are not
  • Anyone's face or voice without their written permission

This protects your business more than it restricts ours. An ad that gets pulled costs you far more than the ad cost in the first place.
