Ask us on WhatsApp

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so every project gets a fixed quote after a free 20-minute call. The quote is one page: what we'll build, the price and the date. No surprises later.

How long does it take?

First ads in about 5 days. Websites launch in 1 to 2 weeks. Software gets a working prototype in about a week. Automations take 3 to 10 days to set up.

Can I start small?

Yes. Start with a one-week project: an ad pilot of 8 to 10 ads for one product or offer, or a working software prototype. You judge our work before committing to more.

Do I pay before I see anything?

You see something working before you pay for the whole thing. We share a first version early, you tell us what to change, and we finish from there.

Where do you work?

Anywhere. We work remotely with clients over WhatsApp, email and video calls, so where your business is based doesn't matter.

Who will I be talking to?

One point of contact runs your project from the first call to launch, so you're never passed around between people.

What happens after launch?

We put it live, train your staff and stay on call. If something needs changing later, message us on WhatsApp.

Can you make ads in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. Reels, product films and posters can be in English, Hindi or Punjabi.

Do you set up WhatsApp auto-replies?

Yes. We set up replies for the questions you answer every day, like timings, menus and bookings, plus alerts when a new enquiry comes in from your website.

Do you use AI?

Yes, to work faster, and you pay for the result. We never make fake reviews, made-up product claims or anyone's face without their permission. Ads with AI-generated people or places carry a small "AI-generated" mark.
